Mean vs median
Estimation · robustness

Mean vs median under heavy tails 平均數與中位數的穩健性

Both estimate the center of a symmetric distribution. With clean normal data the mean is more efficient; a little contamination with wild values reverses that.

x ~ (1 − ε)·N(0,1) + ε·N(0, 10²) efficiency = Var(median) / Var(mean)
  • ε = 0: the median needs about 57% more data (variance ratio π/2 ≈ 1.57).
  • Raise ε: the mean's variance explodes, the median barely moves.
